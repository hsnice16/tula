# 01 · The trade diff

**Status**: planned

## Goal

The code-diff analogue, and the primitive nobody in trading has.

## Acceptance

- Current versus proposed portfolio, with the delta stated in exposure terms.
- Fees, slippage estimate, and the resulting change in liquidation distance.
- Any policy breach listed before the prompt, not after.
- Approve, edit or reject - and reject is the default on timeout.
